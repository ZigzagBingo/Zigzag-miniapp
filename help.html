Help & Support

How can we help you?

How do I play Bingo?

Open Play Bingo, select an available game, join a room and play using your Bingo card.

How do I join a room?

Enter the room code provided by the room host.

Where can I see my games?

Open Game History to see recorded game results when connected.

How does the wallet work?

The current wallet is for demonstration only. Real deposits and withdrawals are not enabled.

Contact Support

For assistance, contact the ZIGZAG Bingo administrator through the Telegram bot.
